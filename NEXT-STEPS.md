# Untamed Entertainment LC — Launch & Marketing Next Steps

The website intentionally does not invent contact details, testimonials, prices, social profiles, reviews, or event photography.

## Information needed to finish the live site
1. Owner's name exactly as it should appear publicly.
2. Real business email.
3. Real business phone number, if it should be public.
4. Final domain name.
5. Real Facebook, Instagram, and Pinterest URLs.
6. Google Business Profile URL after it is created/claimed.
7. Google review link after the profile is active.
8. Real starting prices you are comfortable publishing.
9. Genuine client testimonials with permission to publish them.
10. Approved real event photos.

Add business/contact/social/pricing/testimonial information in:
assets/site-config.js

## Analytics
Create a Google Analytics 4 property and web data stream. Copy the Measurement ID that begins with G- and paste it into gaMeasurementId in assets/site-config.js. The site will then show a consent prompt and load Analytics only for visitors who allow it.

## Search Console
After the final domain is connected, add it to Google Search Console. Domain verification by DNS is the cleanest option because it does not require editing every page. After verification, submit the live sitemap.

## Sitemap
A final sitemap should be generated after the real domain is known so every URL can use the correct absolute domain.

## Google Business Profile
Create or claim Untamed Entertainment LC's Business Profile, complete the service area, business description, services, hours/contact options, real photos, and verification. Then add the real profile and review URLs to site-config.js.

## Reviews
Do not manufacture reviews. After completed events, send clients the direct Google review link. Ask while the experience is still fresh, and reply professionally to every legitimate review.

## Partnerships
Build a small referral network with wedding venues, photographers, DJs, caterers, and event venues in the Liberty/Kansas City area. Give partners a clean one-page service summary and a direct contact method.

## Content engine
Use real event work for Instagram/Facebook, vertical wedding inspiration for Pinterest, service-area landing pages for Google local SEO, and eventually a short email list for event tips, availability announcements, and seasonal offerings.

## Before publishing
- Review privacy/terms with an adult and, if appropriate, a Missouri business attorney/accountant.
- Confirm every service advertised is currently available and properly insured/licensed where required.
- Test every page on a real phone, tablet, laptop, Chrome, Safari, and Edge.
- Compress all new event photos to WebP or AVIF before adding them.
- Replace the contact/config blanks with real information.
