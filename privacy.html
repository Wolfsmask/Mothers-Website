Privacy Policy

How information from this website is handled.

Information you provide

If you contact Untamed Entertainment LC or submit an event inquiry, you may provide information such as your name, email address, event date, location, service interests and event details. This information is used to respond to your inquiry and provide requested services.

Analytics

This website contains optional support for Google Analytics. Analytics does not load unless a real Analytics Measurement ID is configured and the visitor allows analytics through the on-site consent prompt.

Cookies and local storage

If analytics is enabled, the site may use analytics technologies to understand website usage. This includes which pages are visited, how long is spent on them, which navigation links are followed, and which rental items are opened for a closer look. This is recorded to understand which services and items people are interested in. It is not used to identify individual visitors, and none of it is recorded unless analytics is allowed through the consent prompt. The site may also store your analytics-consent choice in your browser so you are not asked on every visit.

Sharing

Untamed Entertainment LC does not sell personal information. Information may be shared with service providers when reasonably necessary to operate the website or provide requested services, or when required by law.

Retention and requests

Business records and inquiry information may be retained as reasonably necessary for operations, legal obligations and customer service. To request access, correction or deletion of information, contact the business using the contact method published on the website.

Policy updates

This policy may be updated as the website, analytics tools or business services change. The current version should be reviewed before public launch and whenever new data-collection tools are added.

Important: This is a general website policy template and not legal advice. Have the final policy reviewed for the business's actual practices before launch.
