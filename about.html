Meet The Owner

Someone Who Notices What Needs To Happen Next.

Untamed Entertainment LC is a Liberty-based event experience company serving Liberty and the Kansas City area. The company is designed around planning, coordination, event support, bartending labor, staffing, rentals and interactive entertainment.

The promise is simple: be prepared, communicate clearly, stay useful when things get busy and help the client spend more time enjoying the event they planned.
