## Open questions

1. **Business name.** The rental agreement is headed **Table Time KC**, but
   the website is built as **Untamed Entertainment LC**. Which is correct, and
   how do the two relate?
2. **Phone number.** The rental agreement gives **913-296-3489**. The website
   config has **(913) 777-4929**. The email matches on both
   (bjolleenm@gmail.com).
3. **Scope of the rental agreement.** It is written specifically around
   tables — "$100 per standard table", "tables must be returned clean". The
   inventory above is far broader, and includes fire, food service and
   electrical items that a per-table clause does not cover.
4. **Insurance**, which she has already flagged herself on the fire pit sheet.
   Fire pit, real-flame candles, cotton candy machine and the chafer fuel are
   the items most likely to need it.
5. **Food handling.** The cotton candy machine is staffed by her on site, and
   the s'mores kit and chafers involve food. Missouri health department rules
   may apply.
6. **Brand names.** Several items are branded (the games, the building bricks,
   the "Now" CD, the toy cars). Renting the physical items is normally fine,
   but naming the brands on a commercial website is a separate question.
   Describing them generically — "plastic building bricks", "party board
   games" — avoids it entirely and costs nothing.
7. **Music and film.** Playing CDs and DVDs at someone else's event can raise
   public-performance questions that are different from simply renting out a
   player.
8. **Where the agreement should live** on the site: a page, a downloadable
   PDF, or something sent privately at booking.
9. **Prices.** None recorded on any sheet or photo, so the pricing section of
   the site stays hidden.
10. **The photographs.** Most are records of stock rather than sales photos:
   dark, shot in a basement, items still bagged, a hand in frame. The catalog
   is built and every item has a photo slot ready, but they need re-shooting.
   See `PHOTO-GUIDE.md`. The lit sign photos are the exception and are good.
11. **One photo is AI-generated.** The wire chafer racks image carries an
   "AI-generated content" watermark, so it does not show the actual racks and
   must not be used to advertise them.
